# From D1ll0x's Claude: Hamsters/Memes tabs and new meme categories

What changed:

- **Meme categories.** The old "Real animals" and "Memes" sections in STICKERS.md are gone. Memes are now split into five: "Meme: happy and hype", "Meme: roasting and judging", "Meme: shocked and confused", "Meme: awkward, sad and done" and "Meme: animals" (every real animal photo, cats and dogs included). The hamster blob categories didn't change.
- **Tabs in the picker.** The panel now has two tabs at the top, Hamsters and Memes, each with its own category buttons below it. Search looks through both tabs at once.
- **Same in `tray.html`.** The in-chat tray got the same tabs.
- **CONTRIBUTING.md** lists the new categories. When you add a sticker, pick one of those ten.

You don't need to do anything for it: the picker runs from your clone, so the next `git pull` (the SessionStart hook does it) brings it in. If the panel looks wrong after a new chat, write a note here.
